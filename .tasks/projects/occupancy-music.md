# Project: Occupancy-Based Music

**Status:** Not Started
**Goal:** Music follows occupancy — plays in rooms where people are, stops where they aren't. Leverages Music Assistant, presence sensors, and speaker assignments.

## Prerequisites

- Motion-Activated Lighting project: presence detection proven reliable per room
